Need a hand? Talk to us ↗

Do I need an app on my phone?

No separate mobile app is needed. Scan the pairing QR code and use the phone webpage in your browser.

Where do files from my phone go?

They save directly in the PC folder you select and grant access to. Open that folder in File Explorer to find them.

Are my transfers encrypted?

Transfers use encryption. The free transfer flow uses a connection service for pairing; it does not upload your files to cloud storage. See the privacy policy for connection and local-storage details.

What do I need to keep open?

Keep Chrome running on your computer and the transfer webpage open on your phone. You can close the extension popup. Pairing needs internet, and some restricted networks can prevent a connection.

How is Pro billed?

The displayed Pro plan is a single $15.59 USD payment for lifetime Pro access, with no monthly subscription.

Is there a file-size limit?

The current free extension supports files up to 500 MB. Transfer time depends on your devices, file size, and network connection.
