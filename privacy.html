Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy covers the PocketDrop extension, phone transfer page, product website, checkout, activation service and support. PocketDrop is provided by Tasnim Akhter, Bangladesh. For privacy questions or requests, contact tasnim454247734@gmail.com.

For technical details about file transfer, device permissions, connection services and local file storage, read Privacy & Connection Details. PocketDrop does not receive or store transferred file contents on its website, payment service or licensing service.

Visiting the website

The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests, which may include IP address, browser information, requested pages and timing. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles these requests under its privacy statement. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to deliver them; see Google's Privacy Policy. Product images and video are served by the website host.

We have not added advertising trackers, analytics scripts or marketing cookies to this website. Payment providers may use their own essential storage and fraud-prevention technology when checkout is opened.

Email and checkout

The Pro dialog asks for an email address to pass to Paddle for checkout and receipts. Typing in that dialog alone does not send the email to Paddle. When you select “Continue to card payment”, the page loads Paddle's checkout software and passes the email and selected price to Paddle. The page does not save your email in browser local storage or add it to the page URL. Closing the dialog clears its fields.

Paddle collects payment, billing and transaction information needed to process orders, comply with legal requirements and prevent fraud. Card details are entered in Paddle's checkout, not a PocketDrop-owned card form. PocketDrop does not receive full card numbers or card security codes. See Paddle's Privacy Notice.

Payments and activation

PocketDrop Pro is sold as a one-time lifetime-access purchase through Paddle. Paddle processes the payment and sends PocketDrop a signed notification when a transaction is completed, refunded or disputed.

For activation, PocketDrop creates a private recovery key and saves it in your browser. Only a hash of that key is attached to the Paddle order. The activation service receives the key over HTTPS to check access and stores its hash, transaction and customer identifiers, price, payment-event timestamps and refund or dispute status. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers and D1. It does not receive transferred files or card details. Network requests expose ordinary connection information such as an IP address to Cloudflare; the IP address is used for rate limiting and is not stored in the PocketDrop licence database.

Activation records are used to deliver lifetime access, restore purchases, provide support, and handle refunds or disputes. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the extension can remove the locally stored key, so users should keep their recovery key private. PocketDrop does not sell activation information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Support and retention

If you contact support, we receive your email and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request. Please do not send card details, passwords, private files or pairing credentials. Support correspondence and purchase records are retained only as needed for support, access administration, dispute handling and applicable legal or accounting obligations. Paddle and the website providers maintain their own retention policies. We do not keep a website database of receipt-email entries.

Sharing and international processing

Data may be handled by the hosting provider, Google Fonts, Paddle, Cloudflare and email providers for the purposes above, including in countries outside your location. We may disclose information when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and rights

You can browse without opening checkout, stop before submitting your email, or contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal data we hold. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations. You may also contact Paddle about payment data it controls. Privacy rights and available complaint routes depend on the laws that apply to you.
