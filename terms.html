The product

PocketDrop is software for transferring files between your own devices. The free Chrome extension supports phone-to-PC and PC-to-phone transfers, QR pairing, encrypted transfers and saving incoming files to a PC folder you choose. Pro adds PC-to-PC transfers, automatic direct connection attempts across compatible networks, files up to 10 GB, same-session resume and recovery-key restoration.
