The product

PocketDrop is software for transferring files between your own devices. The free Chrome extension supports phone-to-PC and PC-to-phone transfers, QR pairing, encrypted transfers and saving incoming phone files to a PC folder you choose. Pro is designed to add PC-to-PC transfers, direct connections across compatible Wi-Fi networks and resuming interrupted transfers.
